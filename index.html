How it works. Lines come from DraftKings Pick6. Each prop's probability blends (a) the no-vig sportsbook price (BetRivers/Kambi, Bovada, and any books from The Odds API if a key is set), shifted to the Pick6 line with a Normal model for yardage-type stats and a Poisson model for count stats, and (b) the player's season average + last 5 games from DraftKings. Value is your edge versus the Pick6 break-even per pick (); multiplier-boosted or discounted picks assume the multiplier scales your payout. Where to play is DraftKings only: Pick6 (main or alt line), plus the DraftKings sportsbook price when The Odds API key is set. The other books are used only to estimate the probability. Models are estimates, not guarantees. Bet responsibly; 21+. If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
