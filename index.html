How to read a card. The big number is the DraftKings Pick6 line. Proj is what the model expects the player to get (▲ above the line = lean More, ▼ below = lean Less). The EDGE tag marks the side the model likes; the % under More / Less is the chance that side hits. The top-right tag is your edge vs. Pick6's break-even ().
